Privacy Policy

Operator: Wata. Last updated: 3 September 2026.

Wata is a local document archive app. Scans, photos, names, notes, signatures, Archive Export credits, and PDFs stay on your device. Wata does not create a cloud account and does not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

  • Document pages you scan or import, including crop, page order, and signature images.
  • Archive titles, types, expiry dates, and notes you type or dictate.
  • Exported PDF files in Wata’s private folder.
  • Purchase records and remaining Archive Export credits. Credits are consumable and stored only on this device.
  • A local login flag so Wata can return to your last draft after you tap Log In.
  • Light preferences such as onboarding complete, last draft, capture mode, and appearance.

Permissions

  • Camera — used when you tap Scan, to capture a document page for a local archive PDF.
  • Photos — used when you tap Import, to copy pages into Wata. Album originals are not edited. Also used when you tap Save preview to Photos. The PDF stays in Wata.
  • Microphone — used when you tap the voice button on a file name, notes, or search field. The keyboard always stays available.
  • Speech recognition — used only with that voice button, to turn speech into text on this device.
  • Tracking — iOS may ask for App Tracking Transparency after you are inside the app. You can decline.
  • Notifications — used to tell you when a local PDF is ready. You can decline.

Wata does not request Camera, Photos, or Microphone on the splash or login screen.

Purchases

Archive Export credits are Apple In-App Purchases. Apple processes payment. Credits live on this device and cannot be restored after you delete the app, Delete Account, or Clear All Local Data. Log Out keeps credits on the device.

Log Out and Delete Account

Log Out ends the local session and keeps archives, media, and credits on the device. Delete Account and Clear All Local Data erase projects, media, Demo history, preferences, and remaining credits on this device after confirmation.
