Wata · on this device

Privacy Policy.

Operator: Wata. Last updated: 4 September 2026.

Wata archives scans, names, signatures, Petals, and PDFs on your iPhone. There is no cloud account. Wata does not sell personal information.

Data on this device

  • Pages you scan or import, plus crop, order, and signature images.
  • Titles, types, expiry dates, and notes.
  • Exported PDFs in Wata’s private folder.
  • Purchase records and remaining Petals. Credits are consumable and stay on this device.
  • A local Enter flag so Wata can return to your last draft.

Permissions

  • Camera — when you tap Scan. iOS shows the system alert.
  • Photos — when you tap Import or Save preview to Photos. Album originals are not edited.
  • Microphone — when you tap a voice button. You can always type instead.

Camera, Photos, and Microphone are not requested on the splash or Enter screen.

Purchases

Petals are Apple In-App Purchases. A Standard PDF export uses 1 Petal. Print quality uses 2 Petals. Preview is free. Credits cannot be restored after you delete the app or Delete Account. Log Out keeps credits on the device. There is no Restore Purchases control.

Log Out and Delete Account

Log Out ends the local session and keeps files and credits. Delete Account erases projects, media, Demo history, preferences, and remaining Petals after confirmation.
