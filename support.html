Wata stores scans, names, signatures, Archive Export credits, and PDFs on this iPhone or iPad. There is no cloud backup in this version.

How to archive a document

  1. Tap Scan or Import, then Confirm pages.
  2. Crop, sort, name, and sign in the editor.
  3. Fill archive details. Preview is free.
  4. Export the PDF. That uses 1 Archive Export Credit.

Permissions

Camera, Photos, and Microphone are requested only after you tap Scan, Import, Save preview to Photos, or a voice button.

Credits

Archive Export credits are stored on this device. They cannot be restored after you delete the app or tap Clear All Local Data / Delete Account.
